The Maccabiah Games

The Maccabiah Games are a big international sports event held in Israel every four years. There are summer and winter sports, and athletes come from all over the world to compete.

אֲנַחְנוּ עַל הַמַּפָּה! "We are on the map!"

Maccabi Tel Aviv is one of Israel's most famous basketball teams. In 1977 the team won the European Champions Cup — the most important club trophy in Europe.

The team was led by Tal Brody, a legendary player who was born in America and moved to Israel. After a huge win, he said the famous words "We are on the map!" — Israel had shown the whole world what it could do.

For Israel, this was much more than a game. It was a moment of national pride that people still remember today.
